Cover for the car you actually drive.

Whether it's a first Polo Vivo or the family bakkie, the right cover depends on how you use it, where you park it and what you can afford if something goes wrong.

Comprehensive
Accident, theft, hijacking, fire and weather damage, plus damage you cause to other people's cars.
Third party, fire and theft
Covers other people's cars, and your own car if it's stolen or burns. A middle ground for older cars.
Third party only
The lowest premium. Pays for damage you cause to someone else's car, not your own.

Towing something? Caravans and trailers can go on the same policy as your car, and so can a second or third vehicle.

Before you start

What should I have with me?

Your ID number, your car's registration number and, if you're insured now, roughly what you pay each month. Everything else is a tap. You can stop halfway and your answers stay on your phone until you come back.

Does it cost anything?

No. Getting quotes is free and you're under no obligation to take any of them.

Why do you need my ID number?

Insurers use your age and driving history to price your cover, and with your permission they run a credit (ITC) check. Your details are used only to prepare your quotes.

How soon will I hear back?

Within one working day, by the method you choose on the form: a call, WhatsApp or email.

Is my information safe?

Yes. Your details are handled under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), shared only with insurers for your quote, and never sold. Read the privacy notice.
